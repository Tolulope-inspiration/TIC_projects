Health & safety

Know when symptoms need urgent care

Do not wait for an online assessment if someone appears seriously unwell. Get help immediately when symptoms are severe, sudden, or rapidly getting worse.

In Nigeria, call 112 where emergency support is available, or go to the nearest emergency department.

Call 112

Get emergency help now if there is:

  • Severe difficulty breathing, blue lips, or severe chest pain
  • Sudden confusion, fainting, seizures, or unusual drowsiness
  • Heavy bleeding, a severe allergic reaction, or signs of stroke
  • Repeated vomiting, inability to keep fluids down, or severe dehydration
  • A rapidly worsening condition or a very high fever in a child

This prototype can support early guidance, but it must never delay professional or emergency care.
