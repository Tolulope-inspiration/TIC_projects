How it works

Simple steps, clear guidance, safer decisions.

1

Describe symptoms

Enter basic information like age, temperature, and the symptoms being experienced.

2

Answer a few questions

Share how long symptoms have been present and whether warning signs are happening.

3

Receive a triage recommendation

The system gives a conservative recommendation based on safety thresholds and clear limits.

4

Follow the recommended next step

Users are guided toward urgent care, prompt review, or routine monitoring where appropriate.

What this system can do

  • Organize symptoms into a structured checklist
  • Offer a transparent triage recommendation
  • Highlight warning signs that need urgent attention
  • Guide users toward appropriate next steps

What this system cannot do

  • Diagnose illnesses or conditions
  • Replace a licensed clinician
  • Guarantee the correctness of a symptom interpretation

Safety notice

Professional care still matters.

This prototype is educational and designed to support early decision-making. It is not a substitute for proper clinical assessment, especially if symptoms are severe, worsening, or persistent.
