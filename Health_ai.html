Early symptom assessment

A faster, safer way to identify when symptoms may need urgent attention.

HealthTriage AI is designed for mobile-first screening of common symptoms seen in everyday clinical practice. It helps people recognise warning signs early, understand when to seek timely care, and decide whether immediate professional attention may be needed.

  • Built for Nigerian healthcare contexts and everyday mobile use
  • Structured symptom checks for safer patient intake
  • Clear escalation prompts for potentially serious conditions
